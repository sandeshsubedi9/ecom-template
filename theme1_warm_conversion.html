Questions

Frequently Asked

Is SynGas compatible with all car models? expand_more
Compatible with any vehicle manufactured in 1996 or later with an OBD2 port — including all cars, trucks, and SUVs.
How long until I see results? expand_more
Most owners notice improvements after approximately 150 miles. Consistent savings are evident after 2-3 fill-ups.
Will it void my vehicle's warranty? expand_more
No. Under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, aftermarket devices cannot void your warranty unless proven to cause damage. SynGas simply optimizes the signal, it does not rewrite or permanently alter the ECU.
